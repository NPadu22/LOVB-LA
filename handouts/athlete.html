Home

Light your five moons

Each day has five habits. Tap one when it's done and its moon lights up. Each moon counts on its own.

Check-in15 seconds: sleep, soreness, energy, mood, stress, and what helped you recover yesterday
Fuel logLog at least one meal in your journal
HydrateTap +1 bottle until you reach your daily ounce goal
RecoverTap what you did: sleep, ice, cold tub, stretch, recovery meal…
Sleep 7h+Tap once you've slept 7 or more hours

Light 3 or more moons for tonight's star. Every Monday, Home shows your Last week summary.

Orbit Performance · LA Orbit12
Good to know

Use it well

Your dataCheck-ins, meals, water and training go to your staff's private sheet. Cycle tracker, notes, photos and allergies stay on your phone. A new phone starts fresh, so keep the same Home Screen icon.
Not medical carePain, swelling, a head hit or anything that worries you: see Emma the same day. The app doesn't replace her or the team doctor.
Fuel questionsSearch Learn › Ask the RD first. Allergies, weight goals, a supplement or a meal plan that doesn't fit: talk to your sports dietitian.
Make it betterSomething broken or missing? Home › App feedback sends a note straight to your dietitian.
